## Experience commitments

- Minimal Structure × Maximum Visual Impact.
- Work appears before a long biography or capability catalogue.
- The first viewport must hook through the new website's own design quality, not through a retrospective case-study claim.
- Motion must explain transformation, pacing, hierarchy, or process.
- Mobile is a first-class art-directed experience, especially for 9:16 media.
- Heavy media must not compromise perceived speed or basic usability.
- The system must support reduced motion, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, semantic structure, captions or transcripts where appropriate, and explicit video controls when audio matters.

## Visual boundaries

- Desired: contemporary, experimental, editorial, cinematic, refined, minimal, unexpected, fashion-forward, and future-facing.
- Avoid: generic AI startup language, cyberpunk, purple-blue gradients, robots, HUD graphics, glow-heavy effects, glass-card dashboards, uniform three-card service grids, and gratuitous cursor tricks.
- The existing high-contrast serif NeoRealm wordmark and rational sans-serif LAB signature are the primary identity anchor.
- The old Web Standardizer yellow, hexagons, skill bars, fixed price cards, and exhaustive thumbnail wall are historical artifacts, not requirements for the new visual system.
